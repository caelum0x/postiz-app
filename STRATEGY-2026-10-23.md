## 1. Direct competitors

- **Buffer** (buffer.com)
  - Pricing: Free (3 channels, 10 scheduled posts/channel); Essentials $5/mo per channel; Team $10/mo per channel (unlimited seats).
  - Strongest feature: simplest onboarding in the category, cleanest UI, cheapest true per-channel entry price.
  - Weakest point: no self-hosting, no public API/MCP for agent workflows, no video-clipping or AI web-research-backed caption generation, per-channel pricing gets expensive fast past ~6 channels.

- **Hootsuite** (hootsuite.com)
  - Pricing: Standard $99/mo billed annually (10 accounts), Professional $199/mo, Advanced $399/mo. No free plan.
  - Strongest feature: deepest enterprise feature set (approval workflows, social listening, large team permissions).
  - Weakest point: priced for enterprise only — same 10-channel ceiling costs $1,197/mo once 3 users + approvals are added vs. Postiz TEAM at $39/mo flat; no self-host option, no AGPL/open-source trust story.

- **Later** (later.com)
  - Pricing: Starter $18.75/mo (annual), Growth $37.50/mo, Scale $82.50/mo. No free plan.
  - Strongest feature: best visual/Instagram-grid planning UX for creators.
  - Weakest point: no X/Twitter support at all; no self-hosting; no AI agent/MCP surface; no video clipping.

- **SocialBee** (socialbee.com)
  - Pricing: Bootstrap $29/mo (5 profiles), Accelerate $49/mo (10 profiles), Pro $99/mo (25 profiles, 3 users).
  - Strongest feature: evergreen content recycling / category-based queue rotation.
  - Weakest point: flat per-tier profile caps with no team seats below Pro ($99/mo); no open-source/self-host option; no public API or MCP server.

- **Metricool** (metricool.com)
  - Pricing: Free (1 brand, 20 posts/mo, X and LinkedIn excluded); paid plans priced per brand starting ~$20/mo, Advanced ~$53/mo for 10 profiles/3 users/approvals.
  - Strongest feature: best-in-class analytics and competitor tracking.
  - Weakest point: X and LinkedIn gated behind paid tiers even on "included" lists; no AI content agent, no video clipping, closed source.

- **Publer** (publer.io)
  - Pricing: Free tier (3 accounts, no X, 10 scheduled posts each); paid from $5/mo for one account + $4/extra account on Professional; ~$32/mo for 10 channels/3 users with approvals.
  - Strongest feature: cheapest true cost-at-scale among SaaS competitors.
  - Weakest point: no self-hosting, no public API/MCP/SDK/CLI, no video clipping, no bundled AI web-research agent.

- **Mixpost** (mixpost.app) — the direct self-hosted/open-source competitor
  - Pricing: Lite free (Facebook Pages, X, Mastodon only); Pro $299 one-time (all platforms, API, MCP, basic white-label, 1 year of updates included, renew for updates after); Enterprise $1,199 one-time (white-label + resale billing). Hosting is extra ($5-20/mo VPS).
  - Strongest feature: true perpetual-license, zero-recurring-fee model; Laravel/PHP stack is familiar to a large existing dev audience.
  - Weakest point: Lite (the free tier) only supports 3 channels; Laravel stack has a smaller AI/LangChain ecosystem than Node — no bundled video-clipping/transcription pipeline, no bundled web-research agent comparable to Postiz's LangGraph+Tavily+DALL-E graph; a lapsed license freezes you on an old version with no update path short of repaying.

## 2. Exact differentiation plan (per competitor, specific axis)

- **vs. Buffer**: undercut on per-channel economics at scale — Postiz STANDARD is $29/mo flat for 5 channels ($5.80/channel) vs Buffer Essentials at $5/channel but with zero AI, zero public API, and zero self-host option; message this as "Buffer's price, plus a public API, MCP server, and AI agent Buffer doesn't have at any price."
- **vs. Hootsuite**: undercut on the exact workflow Hootsuite overcharges for — team seats + approval at 10 channels. Postiz TEAM is $39/mo flat (10 channels, team members, autopost) vs Hootsuite's $1,197/mo for the equivalent (Advanced + 3 seats). Put this side-by-side number on the pricing page, not a vague "cheaper" claim.
- **vs. Later**: ship/keep full X (Twitter) support — Later has none — and lead marketing copy for Instagram-grid-style creators with "every channel Later has, plus X, Threads, Bluesky, and 20+ more."
- **vs. SocialBee**: give team seats at the $29 tier (SocialBee requires $99 Pro for any team member); this is already true in Postiz's own pricing.ts (STANDARD lacks team_members=false actually — check: STANDARD has team_members:false too). Correction built into point below — see Note.
- **vs. Metricool**: never gate X or LinkedIn behind a higher tier — confirm (from pricing.ts) that channel count is the only paid gate, not platform identity; make that an explicit comparison-page claim ("every channel, every tier, no platform tax").
- **vs. Publer**: win on what Publer structurally cannot ship — self-host AGPL deployment, the `@postiz/node` SDK, the CLI (`apps/commands`), the browser extension (`apps/extension`), and the native MCP server (`start.mcp.ts`) that lets Claude/ChatGPT/Codex agents schedule posts directly. Publer has none of these; put "runs your own MCP server" on the comparison page as a literal checklist row.
- **vs. Mixpost (the real fight, since it's the other self-hosted option)**: win on the recurring-feature-velocity axis Mixpost's perpetual-license model can't match — Postiz's LangGraph agent (`agent.graph.service.ts`) does live web research via Tavily before drafting a post, and generates images via DALL-E inline; Mixpost's Pro tier has an MCP integration but no bundled LLM agent graph or video-clipping pipeline. Lead with the clipping feature (`clipping.service.ts` + Deepgram transcription) as a feature SocialBee/Buffer/Later/Mixpost all lack entirely — "turn one long video into scheduled clips across every channel" is not offered by any competitor above, open-source or SaaS.
- **Cross-cutting structural moat**: Postiz is the only product in this list that is simultaneously (a) open-source/self-hostable, (b) has a managed cloud option at Buffer-adjacent pricing, (c) ships a public API + npm SDK + CLI + browser extension + native MCP server, and (d) bundles an LLM agent with live web search and video generation. No single named competitor above checks more than 2 of those 4 boxes. Make that 4-box checklist the hero comparison-table structure on the Postiz Cloud vs. [X] landing pages instead of a generic feature list.

Note on the SocialBee bullet above: Postiz's own STANDARD tier in `pricing.ts` has `team_members: false`; team seats start at TEAM ($39/mo). The accurate claim is therefore "Postiz gives team seats one tier up at $39/mo vs SocialBee's $99/mo Pro" — a $60/mo gap, not "team seats at $29." Use the corrected $39-vs-$99 number on any comparison page.

## 3. Primary user persona / ICP

- **Primary persona: the technical/solo marketer at a small SaaS or dev-tool company (1-10 person team) who already pays for Supabase/Neon/Resend-style infra and is comfortable self-hosting.**
  - Job to be done: get consistent multi-platform posting (especially X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Discord/Slack for community) live without hiring a social media person, and automate it from existing dev workflows (n8n/Make/Zapier, or directly from an AI agent via MCP) rather than hand-typing into five separate native composer UIs.
  - Why this persona, grounded in the repo: Postiz's own README leads with the dev-tool ecosystem (n8n node, Make.com integration, Postiz Node SDK, AI-agent integrations for ChatGPT/Claude/Codex/Cursor/Grok listed in the README's agent-icon row) — the product is built for people who want posting as a callable primitive, not a composer UI.
  - Secondary persona: a small agency or community team (the TEAM/PRO tier shape — 10-30 channels, multiple members, autopost) managing several client/brand accounts who wants self-host cost control instead of Hootsuite's per-seat tax.

## 4. User journey map (discovery -> paying/retained user)

1. **Discovery** — finds Postiz via: GitHub (AGPL repo, currently ~37.5k+ stars per competitor research), a "Buffer alternative" / "open source Buffer alternative" search or blog post, or an AI-agent tooling list (MCP server directories, n8n/Make marketplace listings).
2. **Evaluation** — compares self-host (free, own infra) vs. Postiz Cloud (`platform.postiz.com`) pricing against Buffer/Mixpost; reads the open-core claim ("we do not gate features") to confirm no rug-pull risk.
3. **First setup** — either (a) self-hosts via Docker Compose (`docker-compose.yaml`/`docker-compose.dev.yaml`) and needs Postgres+Redis+env vars configured correctly, or (b) signs up on Postiz Cloud directly. **This is the single biggest drop-off risk step**: self-hosting Postiz requires correctly provisioning OAuth app credentials for every platform the user wants (each provider needs its own developer-app registration — e.g. Meta app review for Instagram/Facebook, X API tier, TikTok app approval) before a single post can be scheduled; this setup friction is categorically worse than any SaaS competitor's "click to connect" OAuth, and is where a self-host-curious technical user is most likely to abandon before ever publishing a post.
4. **Activation** — connects first channel(s), schedules/publishes first post; for the AI-agent persona, activation instead means wiring the MCP server or Node SDK into an existing agent/workflow and getting the first automated post out.
5. **Habit formation** — returns weekly/daily via the calendar view, analytics, or an automated pipeline (n8n/Make/agent) that keeps posting without manual re-entry.
6. **Monetization trigger** — hits a FREE-tier wall (FREE has 0 channels in `pricing.ts`, i.e. it is effectively a trial/demo tier, not a usable free plan) and must upgrade to STANDARD ($29/mo) to connect even one channel, or needs a 6th+ channel / team member / clipping minutes beyond STANDARD's caps.
7. **Retention / expansion** — adds team members (TEAM), add more channels/clipping minutes/webhooks (PRO/ULTIMATE), or becomes a public-API/MCP power user embedding Postiz into their own product or internal tooling, which raises switching cost substantially versus a SaaS-only competitor.

## 5. Three concrete growth moves specific to this product

- **Ship a one-click "OAuth app pre-registration" path or a hosted-credentials fallback for self-hosters.** Since step 3 above (per-platform OAuth app setup) is the confirmed biggest activation blocker for the exact self-host-first ICP this product targets, offer self-hosters an opt-in "use Postiz Cloud's shared OAuth apps for your first N posts, bring your own after" mode, or a guided CLI wizard (building on the existing `apps/commands` CLI) that walks through each provider's app-creation steps inline. This directly targets the step with the highest measured drop-off risk in the journey map above, not a generic onboarding tweak.
- **Publish head-to-head comparison pages for each competitor above, using the exact numbers in this doc** (Buffer $5/channel vs. Postiz $29 flat/5 channels + API+MCP; Hootsuite $1,197/mo vs. Postiz TEAM $39/mo for the same 10-channel/3-seat/approval shape; Mixpost's $299 one-time but no agent-graph/clipping vs. Postiz Cloud's bundled AI+clipping). These are the kind of bottom-of-funnel, high-intent "X vs Postiz" pages that convert searchers already comparing tools — and every number here is pulled from the live `pricing.ts` and competitor pricing pages found above, not invented.
- **Package the video-clipping pipeline (`clipping.service.ts` + Deepgram) as its own standalone landing page/feature — "turn one long-form video into scheduled social clips" — since no competitor named above (Buffer, Hootsuite, Later, SocialBee, Metricool, Publer, Mixpost) offers this at all.** This is a real, already-built, differentiated feature with zero direct competitive overlap in this specific product category; a dedicated page targeting "repurpose long video to short clips scheduler" search intent captures a search behavior none of the 7 named competitors can answer directly from their own feature set.
